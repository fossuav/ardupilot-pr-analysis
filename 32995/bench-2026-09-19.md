# Bench session, 2026-09-19: core1 and core0 load, props off

Andy's analog-OSD quad (RPI_UAVFC), props off on a bench supply, CRSF receiver
on SERIAL3 (PIO UART), bidirectional DShot600, NTSC camera. SWD through a Pico
probe with OpenOCD, all reads non-halting. Every build was the committed
branch code plus an uncommitted hwdef overlay: `AP_RP2350_PC_SAMPLER_ENABLED 1`,
`AP_RP2350_DEBUG_REPORT_ENABLED 1`, `HAL_ENABLE_THREAD_STATISTICS TRUE`.

Motor limits for the whole session: `MOT_SPIN_MAX 0.13`, `ATC_RAT_*_IMAX 0`,
`MOT_HOVER_LEARN 0`, armed only at zero throttle, spin min, or mid-stick while
landed. No output ever went above 1080 us.

Relevant parameters: `INS_HNTCH_MODE 3` (ESC RPM), `INS_HNTCH_OPTS 22`
(per-motor, loop-rate update, triple notch), `INS_HNTCH_HMNCS 7`,
`INS_HNTC2` fixed at 136 Hz, `INS_GYRO_RATE 2` (fast sampling 3.2 kHz),
`FSTRATE_ENABLE 2`, `FSTRATE_DIV 2` (rate loop 1608 Hz), `SCHED_LOOP_RATE 200`,
`EK3_MAG_CAL 4`, `LOG_BITMASK` with ATTITUDE_FAST and PID.

## Method

- **core1 load**: the core1 idle thread's `stats.cumulative`, read over SWD at
  1 Hz against `TIMERAWL`. It is the same number the firmware's `Perf:
  core1load` reports.
- **OSD**: the driver's `late_blocks`, `blank_blocks`, `desyncs` and field
  counters, same 1 Hz reads.
- **core1 profile**: the firmware PC sampler's hash table, snapshotted every
  5 s and zeroed over SWD at the start of each armed window (with Andy's OK;
  it is the profiler's own table). Without the zeroing, armed-only code
  finds the table full of disarmed PCs and its samples are dropped.
- **core0 profile**: DWT PCSR sampled over SWD, about 3900 samples/s, host-side
  histogram, no firmware change and no table to overflow. The firmware
  sampler dropped 50% of core0's samples; this dropped none.
  `Tools/debug/rp2350_pc_profiler.py` on the branch does the same thing for a
  fixed sample count.
- **Main-loop tasks**: `@SYS/tasks.txt` with `SCHED_OPTIONS 1`. The counters
  reset at every `PM` write, so each pull covers the 10 s before it.
- **Across a reflash**, absolute idle is not comparable (PROFILING.md has one
  build at 46% idle on one boot and 67% on the next). The RC relocation below
  is compared as a ratio to EKF3 samples, the method the AP_DAL relocation
  used.

## 2. core1 armed: the harmonic notch coefficient update

Measured on builds that differ only in the change named, same board, same
parameters except `INS_HNTCH_OPTS` in the last column:

| core1 | `202f47e8bb` | + leaf in SRAM `0d16573e5c` | + loop-rate option, `OPTS 18` |
|---|---|---|---|
| disarmed | 51% | 51% | 51% |
| armed, zero throttle | 82% | 66% | 56.5% |
| spin min | 88% | 72% | 61% |
| notch update chain, share of core1 | ~29% | ~10% | ~1.4% |
| late OSD blocks per field, spin min | 0.29 | 0.07 | 0.05 |

**Why the first step worked.** The chain had been relocated
(`d94c26ee20`) without its leaf, `NotchFilter<Vector3f>::init_with_A_and_Q`.
Every re-centre ran the maths from flash and called back into SRAM through
flash veneers for `sinf`, `cosf`, `is_equal` and `constrain`. The maths itself
is single precision with FMA and three divides, about 100-150 cycles; measured
in flash it was about 460 cycles per call, and 80 after the move. 36
coefficient sets (4 motors x 3 harmonics x triple) at 1608 Hz is ~58,000 calls
a second. `linear_interpolate`, which fades a notch below its minimum near
idle, went with it. 512 B of heap.

**Why the second step worked.** The rate thread re-centres the notches at
`filter_rate` = half the gyro rate whatever `INS_HNTCH_OPTS` says. Upstream as
[#34436](../34436/): with the option, unchanged; without it, the scheduler
loop rate. 200 Hz here, so 8x fewer updates.

What is left on core1 armed is the per-sample filter, `NotchFilter::apply`
6.2-6.4%: 36 notches x 3 axes x 3.2 kHz, already in scratch Y. The lever
there is configuration (double rather than triple notch, fewer harmonics).

**What a slower update costs in tracking.** Modelled from the ESC telemetry
of log52 (the per-motor notch flight in DEVELOPMENT.md, 187 Hz median
fundamental; ESC logged at 32 Hz, so peaks are understated): how far the
notch centre trails the motor between updates, against the single notch's
-3 dB half-bandwidth there.

| update | p99 lag | worst transient |
|---|---|---|
| 1608 Hz | 0.2 Hz (1%) | 1.5 Hz (6%) |
| 400 Hz | 0.8 Hz (4%) | 5.8 Hz (25%) |
| 200 Hz | 1.7 Hz (7%) | 11.7 Hz (50%) |

The 5% per-update slew limit caps tracking at ~1870 Hz/s at 200 Hz; 0.07% of
the flight's samples moved faster. `OPTS 18` is fine for ordinary flying;
`OPTS 22` is the choice for hard throttle punches, at ~9 points of core1.

Two readings from earlier in the day are not what they looked like:

- The "58% on the first arm, 82% after" was not an arm state. Aligned with
  the log timestamps, every arm went straight to 82%; 58% was the ~15 s the
  log keeps recording after a disarm.
- Disarmed readings of 28% against 51% were log downloads: with
  `FSTRATE_ENABLE 2` the rate thread drops its rate while
  `in_log_download()`. Disarmed is 51%.

## 3. core0 in ALT_HOLD and LOITER

LOITER with a fake GPS: `GPS1_TYPE 14` fed `GPS_INPUT` at 10 Hz from the
host through Mission Planner's MAVLink mirror. All landed; LOITER only runs
its horizontal position controller once airborne, so this measures GPS fusion
and the landed LOITER path, not the NE controller.

| core0 | ALT_HOLD | LOITER |
|---|---|---|
| busy, disarmed | 74% | 80% |
| busy, armed zero throttle | 80% | 88% |
| busy, armed mid-stick | 82% | 89% |
| `read_AHRS`, mid-stick | 1257 us | 1432 us |
| `update_flight_mode`, mid-stick | 632 us | 897 us |
| `twentyfive_hz_logging` | 420 us | 546 us |
| samples in XIP flash | 61% | 70% |

The two modes were on different boots, so take the busy figures as +/-5
points; the task times are the firmer comparison. The main loop held 200 Hz
with no long loops in any steady armed window (ALT_HOLD session logs, `PM`),
against the field flights' 189-194 Hz and 47-86 long loops per 10 s.

The profile is flat: no function reaches 2%, EKF3's own code is 7-8% of core0
and the rest of the EKF's cost is spread through vector, quaternion and libm
helpers. Software double precision is ~1.6% in LOITER, almost all of it
legitimate position arithmetic (`AC_PosControl::D_update_controller`, the EKF's
`getLLH`/`getPosNE`, `Location`); 64-bit division another 0.66%. While
landed, LOITER re-initialises both position controllers every loop (0.3%
each). The profiling build costs core0 ~2-3% of its own (thread statistics
~2%, the firmware PC sampler ~1%); a flight build does not pay it.

**Angle shaping in double.** `shape_angle_vel_accel()` passed its float angles
through the `postype_t` form of `shape_pos_vel_accel()`, so every attitude
shaping call converted to double and back. Upstream as [#34437](../34437/).
It is correct cleanup but small, about 0.1% of core0 here: most of the double
cost is the position path above.

## 4. The RC input chain into SRAM

The RC chain was ~11% of core0, all of it flash-resident apart from
`ByteBuffer` and the PIO RX service routine: CRSF decode 2.9%, RC channel
processing 2.0%, CRSF telemetry 1.7%, HAL `RCInput` 0.3%, UART and PIO-UART
plumbing for every port 3.6%. That matches the `rcin` thread's 10.4% in
`threads.txt`. `5e55b17873` relocates 63 entries (67 functions, 6.3 KB; 6.6 KB
of heap) - everything at 0.02% or more except CRSF telemetry and the
16 B-or-smaller functions called from flash.

| LOITER armed, core0 | before `982b41d3fe` | after `5e55b17873` |
|---|---|---|
| relocated functions | 6.49% | 1.79% |
| same, relative to EKF3 samples | 0.893 | 0.250 |
| all RC-related code | 10.4% | 5.8% |
| all veneers | 3.49% | 4.40% |
| EKF3 (the reference) | 7.3% | 7.2% |
| busy | 88.5% | 85.4% |

3.6x on the relocated code; veneers into it cost 0.9 points back, spread thin
(the largest is `rc()`, +0.1%). Left in flash: CRSF telemetry, 1.7% over
6.9 KB. The `rcin` thread still polls at 1 kHz for ~150 frames a second.

## 5. Other findings

- **`threads.txt` core1 figures are wrong on SMP.** `Util::thread_info()`
  resets every thread's stats from core0. A thread running on core1 at that
  moment has its measurement start zeroed, so it is credited with the board's
  whole uptime at its next switch. Seen live as a -990% core1 load at arming
  (the logger reads `threads.txt` then), and as core1 idle 2% in a pull
  where the idle counter said 18%. Not fixed; core0 figures are unaffected.
  **Fixed later the same day** on the branch as `c86b75980c` (reset
  `best`, `worst`, `n` and `cumulative`, never `last`). Two more faults in
  the same printout went upstream as [#34438](../34438/) (`d403dceaa5`,
  `b0a6e0ef94` here): the worst-slice mark converted the 1 MHz counter at the
  12 MHz crystal rate, so it only fired over 60 ms, and threads that had not
  run were printed as `LOAD 0.0%*`. Not yet checked on the board.
- **Stalls around log open and close.** A 10 s `PM` window containing a log
  close and the next arm had `MaxT` 457 ms; another after a disarm had 25
  long loops up to 65 ms. Compass and baro go briefly unhealthy (`ERR`) about
  15 s after every disarm, and CRSF dropped 2404 bytes in one such window.
  Ground only so far; not diagnosed.
- **GPS health margin.** `AP_GPS::is_healthy()` wants the average fix interval
  under 215 ms and no two consecutive gaps over 245 ms. A 5 Hz u-blox averages
  200 ms, 15 ms inside the limit, so systematic lateness in reading it trips
  "GPS not healthy", which Andy has seen a lot on this board. `GPA.Delta` in a
  flight log would show it. The fake GPS tripped it at 5 Hz through the mirror
  and was run at 10 Hz.
- **`EK3_MAG_CAL 4` on a stationary bench** gave "compass variance" pre-arm
  failures until a reboot, as the EKF3 playbook describes.

## 6. DCM built out (later the same day)

F8's 1/16 DCM backup is replaced by building DCM out of RP2350:
`AP_AHRS_DCM_ENABLED` 0 in `PICO2.py` (`920c2883a4`), and the four AP_AHRS
decimation commits dropped.

What DCM cost at 1/16, re-counted from the core0 PCSR samples of section 3:
samples in `AP_AHRS_DCM`'s own functions and their veneers, not the vector
and matrix helpers they call.

| window, build `982b41d3fe` | DCM share of core0 |
|---|---|
| ALT_HOLD, armed zero throttle | 0.127% (716 of 562,000) |
| LOITER, armed zero throttle | 0.167% (461 of 276,000) |
| LOITER, armed mid-stick | 0.148% (325 of 220,000) |

At full rate that would be roughly 2.0-2.7% of core0, scaling by 16
(derived, not measured).

What building it out saves, RPI_UAVFC at `0ccbd15f2c` with the profiling
overlay, against the same tree with `920c2883a4`'s two files reversed (DCM
at full rate, since the decimation is gone):

| | DCM on | DCM off | |
|---|---|---|---|
| flash (text) | 1,470,072 B | 1,448,348 B | -21,724 B |
| heap | 309,516 B | 313,100 B | +3,584 B |
| DCM symbols | 42 | 0 | |

`AP_AHRS::update`, in SRAM, is 376 B without DCM (424 B before). Pico2 and
Laurel also build with no warnings and no DCM symbols.

What goes with it (derived from the source, not measured). Copter forces
`FLAG_ALWAYS_USE_EKF`, so DCM was only the attitude before EKF3 starts, the
fallback on an EKF3 `filter_faults`, and the `attitudes_consistent()`
pre-arm check against the one EKF core, the check that failed in
[the round-three video](field-test-2026-09-17-video.md). Without DCM
`fallback_active_EKF_type()` returns EKF3, so a filter fault stays on EKF3
rather than switching to a backup that was 89 deg out after log96. There is
no attitude for the few seconds before EKF3 starts. Not yet booted on the
board; owed: boot, arm, and the OSD horizon in the first seconds.
