# #32995 release-testing flights, 2026-10-31: the compass coupling fell by a third overnight

Two logs from Andy's quad (board `99F517D8`, a modified R3; iFlight BLITZ
E55; u-blox M10 with a QMC5883L on the GPS connector) on `1f8685e9`, the
same build as 2026-10-30, with no parameter changed since log 5 of that day
but the gyro calibration, ground pressure and statistics. The port was
clean. Nothing failed: no EKF failsafe, no emergency yaw reset, and the
EKF yaw within about 6 deg of true throughout. But the compass is no
longer compensated: in Acro it reads 40 deg and more off the EKF's heading
at high throttle, where log 5 read 6-10. The compass's coupling to battery
current fell by about a third between the last flight of 2026-10-30 and
the first of 2026-10-31, so 40-48% of what the refit's `COMPASS_MOT`
applies is now excess. What changed on the airframe is not known.

Logs are named as on the quad's card and never committed. Both have
`INS_ACC_ID` 4456706, `STAT_BOOTCNT` 30 and 31 (following log 5's 29), and
Replay records. Times are `log_extract.py`'s, seconds from the first
message.

## 2. The compass: the refit now over-compensates

### Heading error by load

Compass heading (tilt-compensated with DCM) against EKF yaw, in steady fast
flight (over 8 m/s, rotation under 45 deg/s, roll under 30 deg, pitch -10
to -65 deg) and only where the EKF yaw is within 15 deg of GPS course:

| Median \|compass - EKF yaw\| | 45-55k summed RPM | 55-65k | over 65k |
|---|---|---|---|
| Log 5 Acro (2026-10-30) | 9.9 deg (n 98, 6.4 A) | 6.8 (200, 9.4 A) | 6.5 (150, 14.3 A) |
| Log 6 B Acro | 11.4 (186, 6.2 A) | 25.0 (248, 8.7 A) | 42.1 (52, 16.7 A) |
| Log 7 Acro | 23.1 (76, 8.1 A) | 40.0 (113, 10.7 A) | 43.0 (17, 15.6 A) |

The currents are the median reported in each bin. By reported current,
log 6 B reads 13, 24, 38 and 40 deg at 4-8, 8-12, 12-20 and over 20 A;
log 5 read 9, 6, 5 and 15. Flight 2 (log 3) read 48, 65 and 68 deg by the
2026-10-30 method before its failsafes, so these flights sit between the
two. The error changes sign with heading: compass - GPS course in log 6
read +30 to +44 deg at 737-739 s (heading about 290 deg) and -52 to -107
deg at 750-753 s (about 110 deg), with the EKF yaw within 16 and 6 deg of
course. That is the signature of an error fixed in the body frame.

### The coupling, fitted

`ft_magcoupling.py` fits each flight's logged body field as
earth field (rotated by the EKF attitude) + a body offset + a body vector
per degC of MCU temperature + a body vector per reported amp, by least
squares, and reports the coupling the data implies (the applied
`COMPASS_MOT` minus the per-amp leftover). On log 4, flown with
compensation off, it recovers the interference directly:

| Log | Segment | Compass cal | Implied coupling, mG per reported amp | From `COMPASS_MOT`'s direction | MCU |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Acro before the reset, 221-259 s | earlier | 117.4 at `PERVLT` 50 = 19.9 at 295 | 6 deg | 15.8-22.5 degC |
| 4 | calibration, `COMPASS_MOTCT` 0 | earlier | 20.0 | 7 deg | 13.6-15.2 |
| 5 | 230-455 s | refit | 22.7 | 4 deg | 13.4-16.8 |
| 6 A | 476.5-552.7 s | refit | 14.6 | 26 deg | 16.8-19.6 |
| 6 B | 619.8-835 s | refit | 13.8 | 22 deg | 14.0-21.8 |
| 7 | 88.8-293.5 s | refit | 15.4 | 19 deg | 14.3-23.4 |

Logs 3 and 4 were on the earlier calibration (`COMPASS_SCALE` 1.018, other
diagonals and offsets), so their figures read about 7% high against the
refit's units. Log 4 recovering 20 mG/A within 7 deg of the refit's
direction is the check that the fit measures what it claims. Fit residuals
are 11-24 mG rms per axis.

Today's logs carry a leftover of 10.2-11.9 mG per reported amp along
`COMPASS_MOT` (log 5: 2.1). At 13 A, the p95 of these flights, that is
130-155 mG against an earth horizontal field of 186-191 mG.

The current reading did not change scale. On 0.5 s differences, which drop
slow drift, the pin read 3.36 mV/A against the eRPM model in log 5 and
3.44, 3.82 and 3.45 in logs 6 A, 6 B and 7; against the pack's sag, 74.6
mV per V in log 5 and 54.3, 79.5 and 66.1 (log 6 A is a gentle flight at r
0.82). Put on one scale with either reference, the coupling per true amp
fell 30-40% from log 5. Something between the battery's current path and
the compass changed between log 5 (2026-10-30) and log 6: the pack, its
position or strap, the lead routing, or the GPS mast. Not known; Andy to
say.

### The temperature term

The same fit gives the field's change per degC of board temperature. Along
each log's own coupling, it is what an over-reported current of 0.48
(log 6 B), 0.55 (7), 0.58 (5) and 0.60 (6 A) A/degC would produce, 9-26 deg
from the coupling's direction; log 3's is 0.77 A/degC at the 295 scale, 3
deg from it. The pin's own disarmed drift is 0.76-0.93 A/degC in logs
5-7 (section 3) and 0.64 in log 3. So the compass sees the current zero
moving with temperature, at 60-75% of the pin's disarmed rate in logs 5-7
and 120% in log 3. Over today's in-flight ranges (14.0-21.8 and 14.3-23.4
degC) that is 50-75 mG, about half the per-amp leftover at high load.
Why the in-flight rate differs from the disarmed one is not known: the MCU
sensor may not track the part that leaks as the airflow cools the board.
Mechanism only.

## 4. The GSF in log 7's Loiter, and a tool that misread it

`ft_loiter_yaw.py` put log 7's final Loiter at -27 deg over 180-290 s
(corr 0.71) and -33 deg over 198-278 s (corr 0.69), which reads as a 30
deg EKF yaw error. The GSF agreed in size: EKF - GSF -16 to -35 deg in the
20 s windows of 198-278 s (`ft_imuyaw.py`), with the GSF's own spread
(`XKY0.YCS`) at 0.4-1.4 deg.

It was neither. Rotating the accelerometer's specific force into NED with
the EKF attitude, adding gravity and comparing with GPS acceleration
(`ft_imuyaw.py`) puts the EKF yaw within 1-7 deg of true in every window,
at corr 0.89-0.97. That method reads flight 2's known error after the
260.7 s reset as +53 to +57 deg (corr 0.28, mid-flip), the right sign. So:

- `ft_loiter_yaw.py`'s rotation is not a yaw error here, and drag is the
  likely reason. The command includes the forward acceleration that holds
  speed against drag, which GPS acceleration does not show. Command minus
  measurement at 198-278 s has a median 2.51 m/s/s along the velocity and
  0.10 across it, and the along-track part grows with speed (1.04, 2.09
  and 2.96 m/s/s at 3-6, 6-9 and 9-12 m/s), as drag does. Log 7's Loiter
  was a steady anticlockwise turn (course rate -7 to -13 deg/s at 9-10
  m/s). In a turn the forward term turns the command from the centripetal
  acceleration GPS measures towards the direction of travel, so in an
  anticlockwise turn the measurement reads anticlockwise of the command:
  negative, the sign read. Response lag would read positive there. The
  size is not reconciled: a plain steady turn with these numbers gives
  50-55 deg against the 27-36 read, and the tool weights every sample
  in the window, turning or not. Mechanism only; the accelerometer check
  is what shows the yaw was right. The tool now carries the caveat. Its
  2026-10-30 results (log 3 final Loiter +1 deg, log 5 0 deg) were taken
  at 1-5 m/s, where the along-track term is 0.7-1.5 m/s/s, and agree with
  `ft_imuyaw.py` (log 5 420-440 s: -1.0 deg, corr 0.94), so they stand.
- The GSF was 16-35 deg wrong at 9-10 m/s in Loiter while reporting
  0.4-1.4 deg of spread. That is the overconfidence behind flight 2's yaw
  reset (2026-10-30, "Upstream, not the port"): a pre-failsafe reset taken
  here would have put that error into the EKF. Upstream EKF3 behaviour, not
  the port. Not raised upstream.

## Tools

- `ft_magcoupling.py` - the coupling and temperature fit of section 2.
