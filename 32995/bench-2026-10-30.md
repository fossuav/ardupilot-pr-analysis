# #32995 release-testing flights, 2026-10-30: the compass under motor current, and the current input made usable

Five logs from Andy's quad (board `99F517D8`, a modified R3; iFlight BLITZ
E55; u-blox M10 with a QMC5883L on the GPS connector) on `1f8685e9`, the
release branch carrying #32995 at `e45dae01b1`, the build of 2026-10-29.
Flight 2 (log 3) had three EKF failsafes in Acro and, in Andy's words, "got
the leans partway through". The cause was the compass: motor current
swamped it, and its compensation was too small, for two reasons. The port
itself was clean on every flight. The current input was then rescaled, the
compass refitted from a calibration flight (log 4), and an Acro flight
(log 5) ran clean.

Logs are named as on the quad's card and never committed. Times are
`log_extract.py`'s, seconds from the first message.

Disarmed, the rate thread stalls 1.2-9.8 ms (worst 9.79 ms, log 3 at
115 s). Every stall in logs 2, 3 and 5 falls within 0.3 s of a parameter
save: the `STAT_RUNTIME` save every 30-31 s, arming or disarming, or the
EKF origin being set. RPI_UAVFC keeps parameters in flash with
`AP_STORAGE_NO_WRITE_WHILE_ARMED`, so these stalls happen only on the
ground, which is what that define is for.

of 11568 (8%), the same in all four logs, so the high-water mark is
reached the same way every boot. MSP DisplayPort OSD (`OSD_TYPE` 5) is in
use on this quad. Not investigated.

Every `SERIALn_OPTIONS` is 0, so no port runs half duplex: commit 48's
echo window is still unflown.

## 2. Flight 2 (log 3): the compass under motor current

### Interference

Yaw-independent field components, tilt-compensated with DCM roll and
pitch, samples rotating under 60 deg/s; load bins are motor RPM summed
over the four ESCs:

| Summed RPM | n | \|B\| mG | Horizontal | Down | Applied compensation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| at rest, takeoff spot (160-216 s) | 560 | 400 | 147 | 372 | 8 |
| 30-40k | 102 | 435 | 179 | 389 | 7 |
| 40-50k | 503 | 449 | 207 | 387 | 10 |
| 50-60k | 394 | 457 | 214 | 367 | 13 |
| 60-70k | 118 | 463 | 231 | 330 | 17 |
| over 70k | 27 | 564 | 376 | 336 | 27 |

At rest the field has the right shape for the UK (66-68 deg dip). The
interference left after compensation, fitted against the EKF's attitude
before the yaw reset (221-259 s, earth field from 190-215 s), grew with
load at r 0.91 on X and 0.89 on Z: a median 118 mG in the lower half of
the load, 193-234 mG in the upper half and 481 mG in the top 5%, against an
earth horizontal field of about 150. The applied compensation never passed
27 mG.

Why so small, two reasons multiplied:

- `BATT_AMP_PERVLT` 50 assumes 20 mV/A; the pin gives about 3.4 mV/A
  (section 3), so reported current was 5.8 times low (1.8-4.9 A in flight).
- The old `COMPASS_MOT` was itself wrong: 5.88 mG per reported amp, about
  1.0 mG per true amp, against the refit's 24.7, and pointing 53 deg away
  from it. Its origin is not known. The Acro residual above lies 12 deg
  from the refit's direction and 42 deg from the old one.

### Heading

Against GPS course in fast forward flight (over 8 m/s, nose down 15 deg or
more, roll under 30 deg, rotation under 90 deg/s; 415 samples):

| Summed RPM | under 45k | 45-55k | 55-65k | over 65k |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compass - course, median \|e\| | 27 | 35 | 48 | 84 |
| Compass - EKF yaw, before the reset (221-259 s) | 42 | 48 | 65 | 68 |

EKF yaw - course: median 4 deg, IQR -10 to +17, over the same samples. The
GSF read median -13, IQR -65 to +16. Three of the four takeoffs across logs
2 and 3 logged "ground mag anomaly, yaw re-aligned": the yaw innovation
jumping as throttle comes up is what that check sees.

### The yaw reset at 260.7 s

1. 259.0-260.2 s, 25-27 m/s at 72-81k summed RPM: compass 77-163 deg off
   course, EKF yaw within about 20 deg of it, GSF tracking course until
   259.6 s.
2. Velocity and compass test ratios both over 0.8 (`SV` 1.06-1.47, `SM`
   1.8-2.6). `ekf_check.cpp:68-71` asks for a yaw reset at the eighth
   failing iteration, two before declaring a failsafe.
3. The EKF serves the request from the GSF with `emergency_reset` true
   (`AP_NavEKF3_Control.cpp:889-890`), and `EKFGSF_resetMainFilterYaw()`
   then sets `allMagSensorsFailed` (`AP_NavEKF3_MagFusion.cpp`): the
   compass is out for the rest of the flight (`XKF4.SM` 0 from 260.8 s).
4. The reset landed in a flip: pitch 51-70 deg, roll to 164 deg at
   260.4-261.2 s. The GSF had jumped at 259.8 s (342, 87, 308 deg in
   successive samples) and read 343-345 deg while course was 263-269 deg.
   Its reported spread was 1.5-3.6 deg.
5. After the reset the EKF yaw read 349-350 deg at 23-27 m/s nose down on
   courses of 237-242 deg: about 110 deg wrong. Velocity innovations grew to
   26 m/s by 266 s, and the failsafe ran 265.4-269.1 s. GPS velocity alone
   brought the yaw back to about 30 deg by 270 s.

The other two failsafes followed manoeuvres with the compass already out:
306.6 s after a roll through inverted at 303.6-304.4 s (EKF yaw then 41-58
deg off course, `SV` 2.4, velocity innovation to 7.9 m/s), and 337.8 s
after a 53-63 deg/s yaw turn at 9 m/s (`SV` 2.7). Log 2's Acro flight had
the same chain, smaller: a reset at 700.4 s and the failsafe at 718.0 s
after braking from 24 to 5 m/s (`SV` 5.8, velocity innovation 8.8 m/s).
Acro does not change mode on an EKF failsafe (`FS_EKF_ACTION` 2), so all
four were warnings only.

### The leans

What could be measured:

- 261-263 s, the 110 deg yaw error: EKF roll 8-15 deg against DCM 34-45,
  pitch -33 against -51 to -59. After the 304 s roll, roll 40-80 deg apart
  for about 3 s. Acro has no steady moment to check either against, so
  which horizon was right is not known.
- The final Loiter (381-393 s): EKF roll 1.4-4.5 deg (about 2.5 deg on
  average) right of the tilt the mean specific force gives, in every 2 s
  window; DCM within about 1 deg. Yaw was right: the measured acceleration
  sat +1 deg from the position controller's command (corr 0.40). Log 2's
  Loiter B read within about 1 deg of tilt and -4 deg of yaw (corr 0.63);
  log 2's final Loiter after its Acro +2 deg.
- On the ground and still, the EKF tilt matched the accelerometer to 0.6
  deg after `AHRS_TRIM` everywhere except after log 2's flight A, flown
  with no GPS fix, which came out about 2 deg off in roll.

Andy has not yet said whether the lean was the OSD horizon in Acro or
drift in Loiter.

## 3. The current input on this quad (logs 2-5)

The pin voltage is reconstructed from `BAT.Curr` as Curr / `PERVLT` +
`OFFSET`. Two load references: the `DEVELOPMENT.md` eRPM model
(`I = 0.5 + 9.0e-4 * SUM (RPM/1000)^3`, fitted on the Evoque F5) and the
pack's voltage sag, which does not depend on that model. On 0.5 s
differences, so slow drift drops out:

| Log, flight | r, pin vs eRPM | r, pin vs sag | Gain against eRPM, mV/A | Sag-implied pack resistance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 A | 0.99 | 0.98 | 3.66 | 49 mOhm |
| 2 B (gentle Loiter) | 0.89 | 0.86 | 3.59 | 45 |
| 2 C | 0.98 | 0.98 | 3.47 | 47 |
| 3 | 0.99 | 0.98 | 3.48 | 53 |
| 4 | 1.00 | 0.98 | 3.52 | 58 |
| 5 Acro | 0.96 | 0.99 | 3.36 | 44 |
| 5 final Loiter | 0.96 | 0.96 | 3.10 | 34 |

The sag tracks the eRPM model at r 0.90-0.99, which validates the model as
a relative reference. The absolute scale rests on the model, which on
2026-10-29 read about 30% low against the charger (4.8 A against about
7 A, log 170). If that holds here, the true gain is nearer 2.4 mV/A.

The zero follows board temperature. Disarmed, motors stopped:

| Log | Pin against `MCU.MTemp` | Board range |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 2.89 mV/degC, r 0.99 | 17-26 degC |
| 3 | 2.18, r 0.99 | 15-22 |
| 4 | 1.94, r 0.98 | 11-15 |
| 5 | 2.59, r 0.97 | 13-19 |

At about 3.4 mV/A that is 0.6-0.8 A per degC. Fitting pin = z0 + zT x
temperature + gain x current on a whole log:

| Fitted on | Fit | Zero at 15 / 25 degC |
|---|---|---|
| log 2 | -11.6 mV + 2.65 mV/degC, 3.36 mV/A | 28.2 / 54.6 mV |
| log 4 | -0.2 mV + 1.98 mV/degC, 3.70 mV/A | 29.5 / 49.3 |
| log 5 | -0.5 mV + 2.00 mV/degC, 3.18 mV/A | 29.5 / 49.5 |

Applied to another log unchanged, the log 2 fit put log 3's flight at
5.9 A median against the model's 6.2, 0.85 A rms. The log 4 fit put log 5's
final Loiter at 4.2 A against 4.6, 0.40 A rms, and its Acro at 10.4 against
12.2, 2.65 A rms.

The battery voltage input shows no comparable drift: in log 5, disarmed, it
held 25.51-25.53 V while the board went from 15.4 to 18.7 degC, under
0.3 mV/degC at the pin. The drift belongs to the current input, which
fits the leaky clamp on these boards. On 2026-10-29's bench, though, the
battery pin drifted 1.70 mV/degC too, so R4 needs measuring before this is
called fixed. Switching relay 2 (the 5 V VTX rail) moved the pin by at most
1.6 mV in any log.

Consumed capacity per pack:

| Pack | Logged | eRPM model | Temperature-corrected pin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Log 2, three flights, 25.54 to 22.50 V | 458 mAh | 871 mAh | 876 mAh |
| Log 3, 25.54 to 23.49 V | 176 | 405 | 370 |
| Log 4 (rescaled), 25.53 to 24.73 V | 10 | 155 | |
| Log 5 (rescaled), 25.52 to 22.83 V | 578 | 753 | |

Log 4's 10 mAh is the disarmed current reading negative on a cold board
(-1.5 A at 11-15 degC against the 35 mV offset), cancelling the flight's
90 mAh. Charger figures for these packs would anchor the scale.

## 4. Rescaled and refitted (logs 4 and 5)

at about 17.5 degC). Andy then refitted the compass for log 5.

164 mG at hover to 108 at 50-60k and 66 at 60-70k summed RPM, with the

Against course, log 5's compass still reads 25 deg off at 45-55k, but the
EKF yaw reads 23 deg off there too: at low throttle in Acro the quad
slides, and course is a weak heading reference. Against the EKF the only
leftover is the lowest bin, under 45k: 22 deg from 24 samples. `XKF4.SM`
reached 3.70 in places (p95 0.50).

### What a stable current zero would add: not testable from these logs

Recomputing log 5's compensation as `COMPASS_MOT` x a temperature-corrected
current, or x the eRPM model, makes the heading worse (compass - course
median -6.9 and -10.5 deg, IQR -25 to +16 and -31 to +19), not better. The
refit was made against the current as it read at log 4's board
temperature, zero error included, and its offsets absorbed it (Z moved
146 mG). The as-flown setup is self-consistent, so the benefit of a
stable zero can only be measured by refitting on fixed hardware.

### The risk until then

The calibration flight and the test flight ran at the same cool board
temperature, 11-19 degC. Each degC away from it moves the zero 0.6-0.8 A,
which with 24.7 mG/A of `COMPASS_MOT` is 15-20 mG of compass error per
degC. A board 10 degC warmer would put back interference of the size that
broke flight 2. Disarmed, the reading should sit near the board's real
draw of a few tenths of an amp; ±1.5 A means the zero has moved.

## 5. Other findings

- Magnetically distorted resting spots. Wherever the quad was powered up
  in log 3 read 286 mG horizontal and 273 down (44 deg dip), and log 4's
  read 250 and 348. The spot before log 2's second flight read 275 and
  338. Log 2's landing spot after flight C read 182 and 327. There the
  compass heading moved about 150 deg as the motors stopped, with no
  rotation (yaw rate under 26 deg/s through the e-stop landing), and the
  EKF yaw was within 2 deg of true in the Loiter just before. The EKF
  aligns yaw at boot on whatever the compass reads there.
- GPS time to first 3D fix: log 2 471 s, log 3 7 s, log 4 116 s, log 5 7 s.
  The card's file times put about 10 minutes off before each fast fix and
  about 35 minutes or more before each slow one: a receiver that keeps its
  state for a while. Inferred from file times, not measured.
- Log 2's flight C ended with the motor e-stop switch at about 0 m.

## Upstream, not the port

Copter's pre-failsafe yaw reset is served from the GSF with
`emergency_reset` true, which also fails the compass for the rest of the
flight. In flight 2 it fired mid-flip, just after the GSF had lost track in
the same manoeuvre while reporting a spread of a few degrees, and turned a
20 deg yaw error into 110 deg. That is EKF3 and Copter behaviour on any
board. Not raised upstream.

## Withdrawn or corrected on 2026-10-30

- "The old `COMPASS_MOT` had about the right shape and was only starved
  by the current reading" (from residuals 25 deg off it in hover and 42 deg
  in Acro). The refit points 53 deg away from the old values and is about
  24 times larger per true amp. The hover residual fit (r under 0.2) said
  nothing; the Acro one is 12 deg from the refit.
- "The current input reads 1/7 to 1/10 of true current" (from 2026-10-29's
  2-3 mV/A). Measured at 5.8 times low against the eRPM model: 3.4 mV/A
  against the 20 mV/A that `BATT_AMP_PERVLT` 50 assumes.
- "After log 2's last landing the EKF yaw was about 180 deg wrong." The
  landing spot was distorted.
- "A temperature-corrected current would improve log 5's heading." Not
  testable without a refit; see section 4.

## Tools

In `tools/`, run under WSL with numpy and pymavlink. `logpickle.py` reads a
log once into a pickle of arrays and the rest read that pickle:

- `logpickle.py` - selected messages to numpy arrays, `log_extract.py`'s
  time base.
- `ft_timeline.py` - 1-2 s table of GPS, compass, EKF and attitude.
- `ft_detail.py` - 5 Hz yaw, compass, course and innovations in a window;
  `hover` mode for the specific-force tilt check.
- `ft_magcheck.py` - yaw-independent field components by load and heading
  against GPS course.
- `ft_magresid.py` - uncompensated interference in the body frame against
  load, and its direction against `COMPASS_MOT`.
- `ft_compcf.py` - the compensation counterfactual of section 4.
- `ft_currcheck.py` - the current pin against the eRPM model and sag, the
  disarmed zero against temperature, relay steps.
- `ft_currmodel.py` - fit zero(T) and gain on one log, predict others
  (`PERVLT`/`OFFSET` from the environment).
- `ft_tilt.py`, `ft_tilt_scan.py` - EKF and DCM tilt against the
  accelerometer on still and quasi-steady windows.
- `ft_loiter_yaw.py` - EKF yaw error from the rotation between commanded
  and GPS-measured acceleration in a position mode.
- `ft_porthealth.py` - loop timing, EKF timing, drops, ESC, GPS per segment.
