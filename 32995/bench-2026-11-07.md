# #32995 release-testing flights, 2026-11-07: the first flights at 200 MHz

One log, two flights, from Andy's quad (board `99F517D8`, a modified R3;
iFlight BLITZ E55; u-blox M10 with a QMC5883L on the GPS connector) on
`4d4220d9`, the alpha2 branch carrying #32995 at `f0f78b48ec`; the PR's
head is now `1fcec947c5`, with an identical tree. These are the first
flights of what that head brought, all of it bench only until now: 200 MHz clocks, 100 MHz continuous-read flash, the new SRAM placement
on both cores, external AHRS compiled out, SPI at the fastest clock not
above the request (the microSD at 25 MHz), and fractional PIO dividers for
bidirectional DShot and the OSD.

Parameters are log 5's of 2026-10-30 but for the gyro calibration, ground
pressure and statistics, and `EAHRS_TYPE`, which the build no longer has
(`ft_pdiff.py`). So against log 5 the build is the only change.

The port was clean in flight and the scheduler load fell by about 25
points against log 5. One logging drop, 0.3 s on the ground after the last
disarm, is not explained. RTL flew for the first time on the port. The
compass's coupling to battery current is back near log 5's, and the
current input's temperature drift, through `COMPASS_MOTCT` 2, turned the
heading on the ground and failed the mag-field arming check.

Logs are named as on the quad's card and never committed. `STAT_BOOTCNT` 34
(log 7 of 2026-10-31 was 31), `INS_ACC_ID` 4456706 as before, Replay
records present. Times are `log_extract.py`'s, seconds from the first
message.

Disarmed, log 5 read 56.7-58.2% load with 56 long loops in 20-200 s and a
worst rate-loop gap of 5.03 ms; log 8 reads 31.4-32.9% with no long loops
at any time, and the rate thread stalls 1.5-4.3 ms at each 31 s statistics
save and when home is set, and 4.7-5.1 ms at each disarm. Free heap (`PM.Mem`) is 157.3 KB at the
median and 151.6 KB at the lowest, against 163.3 KB in log 5: about 6 KB
less, which fits code moved into SRAM. `STAK` is unchanged: the OSD thread
still has 104 of 1504 bytes free.

Both builds carry the release branch's two load measurement commits, so the
load figures measure the same thing. The bench (HISTORY.md, 2026-11-07) put
#32995 at 36% at 225 MHz, 31% at 200 MHz and 25% with the placement and
external AHRS out, a fall of 11 points; on the quad it is about 25. Why the
quad gains more is not known.

Bidirectional DShot on the fractional divider: telemetry from all four ESCs
at 80-85 Hz with no errors. At each e-stop the outputs went to 1000 and
the RPM to 0 within 0.4-0.5 s, and on release the motors were back at
spin-arm RPM within 0.5 s. The OSD on its fractional divider cannot be
judged from the log.

### Superseded 2026-11-07 by the quad's `OSD_TYPE`

The PIO OSD did not run in log 8, so the fractional divider was flown by
bidirectional DShot only. The quad has `OSD_TYPE` 5 (MSP DisplayPort) and
`OSD_TYPE2` 0; the PIO OSD is `OSD_TYPE` 6 (`OSD_PICO`, `AP_OSD.h`). The
introduction's "fractional PIO dividers for bidirectional DShot and the
OSD" and the Open item "needs the video" read as if it had. Checking it
needs a board with analog video and `OSD_TYPE` 6. The text above is left
in place because the log indeed cannot judge it.

### The logging drop

At 1129.26-1129.56 s, 1.4 s after the last disarm, every logged stream
lost 0.3 s: two rows of each 10 Hz message and 18 of `IMU`. `DSF.Dp` went
from 0 to 281, `FMn` to 0, and the `DSF` row due at 1129.4 s is missing.
The rest of the system ran on: `PM` over 1120-1130 s has no long loops and
`MaxT` 5.13 ms, and the rate thread has no window over 1 ms after 1127.96
s. So the writer stopped draining the buffer: the 34 KB free at 1128.4 s is
0.5 s of the 69-70 KB/s being written, which puts the stall at about 0.8 s
(derived from the buffer figures).

A boot-flash write is ruled out: its park holds core0's kernel lock through
an erase and stops core1 (`rp2350/DEVELOPMENT.md`, "Flash writes park
core1"), and neither loop stalled. Left are the card's own write latency
and the 25 MHz SPI clock, which alpha2 changed. Logs 2-7 on `1f8685e9`
dropped nothing, at the same `LOG_BITMASK` (442366). One event, on the
ground: watch `DSF.Dp` in the next logs.

## 3. The current zero on the ground, through `COMPASS_MOT`

The board sat powered for 15 minutes waiting for the GPS and warmed from 12
to 25 degC. The current pin's zero moved 2.72 mV/degC (r 0.99,
`ft_currcheck.py`), as in logs 5-7 (2.59-3.15). At `BATT_AMP_PERVLT` 295
that reported -2.9 A at boot, 4.4-4.9 A at 600-720 s and 6.4-7.2 A at
840-900 s, all with the motors stopped. `COMPASS_MOTCT` 2 applies 24.7 mG
per reported amp, so the logged motor offset (`MAG.MO`) was 71, 109-120
and 157-176 mG.

`ft_magarm.py` rebuilds the arming check (`AP_Arming::compass_checks()`:
the field rotated to NED by the AHRS, minus the earth field, max of |N| and
|E| against `ARMING_MAGTHRESH` 150), using flight B's fitted earth field
(192, 0, 450 mG):

| Window | MCU | Reported A | MO | xy diff | Without MO | Heading shift from MO |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-30 s | 12.1 degC | -2.9 | 71 mG | 10 | 56 | -8.5 deg |
| 270-300 s | 20.8 | 2.4 | 59 | 99 | 54 | +4.8 |
| 600-630 s | 22.7 | 4.4 | 109 | 157 | 78 | +10.5 |
| 660-690 s | 23.3 | 4.9 | 120 | 165 | 77 | +11.3 |
| 720-750 s | 23.1 | 4.9 | 119 | 46 | 68 | +24.2 |
| 870-900 s | 25.1 | 7.2 | 176 | 134 | 64 | +20.0 |

The rebuilt 157-165 mG matches the logged 151-162. Without the
compensation it would have read 76-78. The check cleared at 720 s when the
quad was turned from about 345 to 304 deg, and armed at 900.5 s with about
134 mG.

On the ground the EKF yaw follows the compensated compass (heading fusion
before the first climb), so the shift moved the yaw with it: -8.5 deg at
boot, +11 deg at 600-720 s and +20 deg by arming. The in-flight alignment
at 908.0 s, with 14 A of real current, moved the EKF yaw 10 deg (351.2 to
1.3); logs 6 and 7's moved it 6-10. The true heading on the ground is not
known, so how much of the 20 deg was error is not measured.

This is the known limitation of the R3 current input seen from a new side:
through current-based compensation, a warm or cold board turns the heading
on the ground, and the longer it sits powered the more. Section 5 has a way
round it.

## 5. Throttle-based compensation would not see the drifting zero

`ft_magregressor.py` fits the uncompensated field (`MAG` minus `MO`) with
each load signal in turn, and reports the residual and the heading error it
leaves:

| Load signal | Log 8 B | Log 5 |
|---|---|---|
| none | 51.1 mG rms, heading median 10.3 deg, p90 29.4 | 65.5, 11.0, 35.2 |
| reported current | 22.4, 4.7, 12.9 | 20.9, 4.6, 11.8 |
| reported current with a temperature term | 18.3, 4.2, 11.0 | 19.4, 3.9, 11.3 |
| throttle, `CTUN.ThO` | 20.0, 4.6, 12.6 | 24.2, 5.2, 14.8 |
| summed eRPM cubed | 20.1, 4.7, 12.5 | 26.7, 5.8, 17.2 |

`CTUN.ThO` is `motors->get_throttle()`, what Copter hands the compass
(`Copter.cpp`) and what `COMPASS_MOTCT` 1 multiplies by `COMPASS_MOT`.
Throttle explains the interference to within a few mG of current, and it is
zero while disarmed and at spin-arm, so it would remove section 3's ground
shift and the cold under-reading in flight. The values it implies, minus
the fitted coefficient, are (1066, -202, -718) mG at full throttle from log
8 B and (1114, -421, -763) from log 5. X lies beyond the parameter's
documented range of -1000 to 1000, which nothing in `AP_Compass` enforces.
Copter's `compassmot` uses current whenever the battery monitor reports
it (`compassmot.cpp`), so throttle values would come from a fit like this
or from a flight with `COMPASS_MOTCT` 0. Not flown.

## 6. Other findings

- RTL, its first flight on the port, 1095.5-1109.5 s: 33.7 m from home at
  19.7 m, against `RTL_ALT_M` 20, so no climb. It flew home at up to 11.6
  m/s against a velocity target of up to 22.7 m/s (`WP_SPD` 40 with
  `RTL_SPEED_MS` 0), overshot by
  3.4 m, held within 1 m of home through the 5 s `RTL_LOIT_TIME`, and began
  its descent at about 1107.5 s. Andy switched to Loiter at 1109.5 s at
  about 11 m, so the final descent and the landing did not fly.
- The three "GPS Glitch or Compass error" flags were in Acro at 14-21 m/s,
  as log 5's and log 6 B's. EKF3 raises the flag when the receiver's speed
  accuracy, low-passed and peak-held, exceeds 1.5 m/s, or when the GPS
  velocity or position test ratio stays at 1 or more for over a second
  (`calcGpsGoodForFlight()`). At 933.5 and 987.5 s the receiver reported
  1.98 and 1.87 m/s while `SV` stayed under 1 (0.87, 0.92), so the receiver
  raised those two. At 996.9 s it reported 2.32 m/s and `SV` reached 1.76,
  `SM` 4.78, velocity innovation 11.5 m/s; the flag took 10.6 s to clear.
  `EK3_GLITCH_RAD` 0, so no reset.
- The radio failsafe at 31.1-32.8 s, disarmed, fell between "CRSFv2: Link
  rate 100Hz" at 28.8 s and "ELRS: Link rate 333Hz" at 33.8 s: the link
  changing packet rate after connecting. Log 7's ground failsafe of
  2026-10-31 may be the same; not checked.
- `VIBE` in flight B: X/Y/Z mean 16/17/13 m/s/s, peak 86, no clipping.
- Every `SERIALn_OPTIONS` is still 0, so commit 48's echo window is still
  unflown.
