# #32995 bench, 2026-10-01: lock-free microsecond clock, vector tables, SWD

RPI_UAVFC on COM9, picoprobe on SWD, app at `0x10020000`. Windows OpenOCD
(`/opt/openocd-0.12.0+dev-x64-win`) driven from WSL through its TCL port;
MAVLink through Windows `python.exe` with pymavlink staged in
`C:\tmp\claude-bench\pylib`. Bench helpers (`tcl.py`, `collect.py`,
`reboot.py`, `ftpget.py`) are in `C:\tmp\claude-bench`.

## 1. Lock-free `hrt_micros64()` A/B

Prompted by Matt pointing at Betaflight #15619, which gives the Pico port a
cheap `millis()` by running TIMER1 at 32 kHz. Rather than a second timer,
`hrt_micros64()` on RP2350 now reads TIMER0's raw 64-bit count
(`TIMERAWH`, `TIMERAWL`, `TIMERAWH` again, retry if the high word moved)
plus `AP_BOARD_START_TIME`, with no lock. `millis()` stays
`micros64 / 1000`. `hrt_micros64I` is compiled out on RP2350. The image is
112 B smaller.

A is the PR head before the change, B adds it. Cycle counts from DWT
around each call on core0:

| measure | A (`port_lock` + 32-bit wrap) | B (lock-free) |
|---|---|---|
| `micros64()` per call | ~90-94 cycles | ~37 cycles |
| `millis()` per call | ~126-132 cycles | ~78 cycles |
| calls over 200 cycles (waited on the lock) | 0.6-1.9% | 0.1-0.3% |
| TIMER0 against the ChibiOS time stamp | 0-1 us | 0-1 us |
| main loop | 200 Hz | 200 Hz |
| core0 load | 40% | 40-41% |

Per call it is a clear win. At the system level nothing measurable moved:
loop rate and load are the same within the noise. Both builds swing from
boot to boot between two states, rate ~2027 Hz at ~40% load or
"rate=4000Hz" at 0% load, independent of the build. Not explained.

Committed on its own (`805070fb5f`, now `2d147f576d` "hwdef: read the
RP2350 microsecond clock without the kernel lock") so it can be dropped
without touching anything else. This also closes the 2026-09-14 audit
item "`hrt.c` uses `port_lock()` on RP2350 ... safety under SMP was not
checked": there is no lock left to check.

## 2. The hang after an SWD flash

During the A/B, B hung 4 times out of 4 after an SWD flash followed by an
OpenOCD detach while it booted; A 0 of 4. Core1 sat in the bootrom at PC
`0x0000019e` (LR `0x00000203`), never launched; core0 around `0x1017cfb2`
(`rpEflAfterXipOn`), waiting on core1 for each flash operation. No hang
with the debugger left attached, on 5 of 5 MAVLink reboots, or on clean
power-ups. Andy then power-cycled B four times without a hang and it was
committed.

Seen again on 2026-10-01 17:17 with the PR head `564e6c1c7d`, flashed over
SWD: core1 VTOR 0 and PC `0x19e`; core0 halted twice at `0x1017cefa` in
`rpEflBeforeXipOff`, the 10 ms wait for core1 to acknowledge the XIP
lockout. MAVLink still answered. A MAVLink reboot then did **not** clear
it: core1 was back at `0x19e`, and after a second OpenOCD session the
board stopped sending heartbeats. A power cycle cleared it.

**Correction:** section 1's "no hang on MAVLink reboots" was taken on
boards that had booted cleanly. Once a board is in this state, a MAVLink
reboot does not get it out.

So far every occurrence follows an SWD session and none follows a power-up.
Hypothesis, untested: OpenOCD leaves debug state (DHCSR `C_DEBUGEN`) set in
the core after it detaches, and a reset short of a power cycle then fails to
launch core1. Bench rule until that is settled: after an SWD flash, power-cycle
the board before trusting it.

## 3. Vector tables, read off the running board

`c1_main.c` was cut down on 2026-10-01 and the vector tables moved into
uncontended SRAM: core0's to `.c0_vectors` at `0x20080000` (SRAM8), core1's
to `.c1_vectors` at `0x20081000` (SRAM9). The first version of that told
Andy core1 ran from the SRAM9 table, having checked only where the table
was placed. `_crt0_c1_entry` writes the flash `_vectors` (literal
`0x10020000`) into VTOR before it calls `__c1_cpu_init()`, so core1 was
still dispatching from flash. `__c1_cpu_init()` now sets VTOR itself.

Checked at the head `564e6c1c7d` after a power cycle, attached without
halting:

| core | VTOR | PC (DWT PCSR `0xE000101C`) | symbol |
|---|---|---|---|
| core0 | `0x20080000` | `0x20001024` | `rp2350_vectors` / `rp2350_idle_c0` |
| core1 | `0x20081000` | `0x1017c186` | `rp2350_c1_vectors` / `chEvtWaitOne` |

Core1 DHCSR `0x01100001`: debug enabled, not halted. MAVLink answered
before and after. Reading PCSR on each core's AP gives a PC without halting,
which matters here: halting core1 stalls core0's MAVLink.

## 4. PIO UART per-port lock on hardware

`564e6c1c7d` replaces the kernel lock in PIOUART with a per-port atomic
lock (see the README). Andy flashed it to a second board and u-blox
detection on the PIO UART works. The cross-core case it fixes (a FETtec
purge from core1 while the interrupt runs on core0) is not exercised by
that.

### Superseded 2026-10-02

That check did not test the lock: the u-blox is on hardware UART1, and
SERIAL3 (the PIO UART) is the ELRS receiver. The lock deadlocked in Andy's
flight build within seconds; see [bench-2026-10-02.md](bench-2026-10-02.md)
section 1.
