## What the Q25 logs contradict

- **#34942's static yaw reference survives a filter reset.** `3c73e8b64b`
  takes the reference "once at the start, as a vehicle still from its first
  sample would otherwise fuse an unset reference". After log 2's disarmed
  "EKF3 IMU0 forced reset" (98.57 s, see 34942/README.md) the vehicle was
  still, and neither `InitialiseVariables()` nor `InitialiseFilterBootstrap()`
  clears `yawAngDataStatic` or `onGroundNotMoving`. The reset filter, at about
  360 deg, fused the pre-reset reference: yaw ran back to exactly 332.4 deg by
  103.3 s and on to 324.6 deg by 106.9 s, while the raw Z gyro never read over
  0.57 deg/s, and XKF1.GZ was learnt to 3.74 deg/s, still 1.74 deg/s at
  204.9 s. The vehicle armed at 106.16 s with that bias. Replay reproduces it
  exactly. The capture in upstream EKF3 has the same shape, so the gap is
  probably upstream's too; on this branch it is reached through #34942
  making a ground height error trigger the forced reset.
- **The log tools' mode name.** Mode 29 is VALT on SFD; the earlier note
  for these logs should not read RATE_ACRO.
